Abandoned House

This project was my first experience diving a little deeper into some of Blender's more complex features. I followed Blender Guru's abandoned house tutorial, with the goal of developing my skills in mesh creation and knowledge of what goes into designing and creating an environment. In the process, I learned a lot about shader nodes, UV editing, and texture painting. This project was great practice building more complex meshes using the simple geometry building blocks and modifiers that blender provides. The decorative gable ornaments under the peaks of the roof, for example, seem simple but caused me a lot of frustration at the time.

I am particularly proud of the dynamic textures on the house's walls: they use painted RGB channels on an image mask to correspond to different shaders. This effect, paired with a color burn color mixer, allows the wood texture to almost seamlessly blend between painted wood, peeled wood, and moss, as well as a water damaged overlay. This technique was also used on the house foundations, porch, ground, and roof to add depth to an otherwise flat texture. This technique was incredibly useful to learn, and I still make use of it to add depth to otherwise flat or repetitive textures. In addition, I explored the basics of particle systems to create the environment around the house, layering different kinds of grass and trees to add depth.

In the future, I'd love to put more effort into the scenery around the house, diving more in-depth into particle systems and creating more variety in the foliage. I am satisfied with the grass at a beginner level, but it leaves a lot to be desired for realism.

Isometric Bedroom

I began this project following 3DGreenhorn's tutorial for making an Isometric Room. However, this was my first time really experimenting with Blender's interface and creating things on my own, so I quickly began straying from the tutorial. I imported assets from BlenderKit for the keyboard, mouse, PC, and chair, as well as certain materials such as the wood and linens but aside from that everything is my own.

I learned a lot about the basics of UV editing, material creation such as the transparent glass and light-emitting donut lamp, and I scraped the surface of Blender's physics features while making the curtains.
